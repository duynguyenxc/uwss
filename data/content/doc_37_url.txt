ABSTRACT
In chapter 2, the corrosion of steel buried in concrete was studied and it was found 
that the corrosion has the same characteristics in terms of its impact on steel in any 
situation. The causes of corrosion or its types that affect the steel have been studied 
in detail in chapter 3. When we see that a concrete structure has signs of corrosion, 
we want to repair it, but before we do that, we must evaluate the building with high 
accuracy and identify the environmental conditions surrounding it.
